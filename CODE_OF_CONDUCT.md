# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Rotor Rush welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give actionable feedback and accept it gracefully.
- Focus disagreement on ideas, code, and player experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's contact channel. All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
